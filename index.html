Boards

The microcontrollers and computers for your next stage — from the 5V Arduino UNO to the ESP32 family and the Raspberry Pi. Each board lists its specs, the pins that matter and the mistakes to avoid. Tick the boards you own; the projects use them.

5V vs 3.3V — the rule that matters most

Arduino UNO, Nano and Mega use 5V signals. ESP32, ESP8266 and Pico use 3.3V and can be damaged by 5V on a pin. From a 5V module into a 3.3V board use a voltage divider (e.g. 1kΩ + 2kΩ) or a level shifter; for I2C between 5V and 3.3V use a bidirectional BSS138 level shifter.
